**half wave plate** - rotates incoming light by twice the angle between the incident polarization and the crystal's optical axis
- rotated within +/- 54 µas of the command
- polarimetric efficiency is plotted for different HWP angles in Fig. 6.

**Wollaston prism** - splits incoming light into to beams of orthogonal polarization, the **ordinary beam** and **extraordinary beam**
- These get projected on to the top and bottom of the CCD.

**precision callibration unit (PCU2)** - used for astrometric callibration
- contains a mount that holds one of the following: pinhole mask, HWP for JHK observing, and a HWP for L' observing
- optics must be manually switched by staff

The linear stages in the PCU2 can move in the x and y directions.
- The HWP's and pinhole mask are located on the upper z stage, while a calibration fiber is located on the lower z stage.
