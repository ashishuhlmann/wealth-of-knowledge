## 9/28/26

Magnetic fields come from moving charges.

The two ways to generate current are
1. **convection current** - shift a charged body in space
2. **galvaic current** - apply a potential difference accross two ends of a conductor
3. **displacement current** - change the strength of an electric field in space
### Continuty Equation

1. integral form
$$
\int_{V}\frac{d\rho}{dt}dV+\int_{\delta V}\textbf{j}\cdot d\textbf{A}
$$
2. divergence form
$$
\frac{d\rho}{dt}+\nabla\cdot\textbf{j}=0
$$
### Lorentz Force

The total force on a charge $q$ moving at a velocity $\textbf{v}$ is
$$
\textbf{F}=q(\textbf{E}+\textbf{v}\times\textbf{B})
$$
