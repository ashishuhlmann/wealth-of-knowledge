## 4/22/26

The potential of a dipole of charges $\pm q$ at separation $l$ at a large distance $r\gg l$ is
$$
\phi=\frac{ql\cos{(\theta)}}{4\pi\epsilon_{0}r^{2}}
$$
- $\theta$ is the angle measured from the axis of the dipole.
- $p=ql$ is the **dipole moment**.

The electric field is
$$
\textbf{E}=\frac{ql}{4\pi\epsilon_{0}r^{3}}(2\cos{(\theta)}\hat{\textbf{r}}+\sin{(\theta)}\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}})
$$
