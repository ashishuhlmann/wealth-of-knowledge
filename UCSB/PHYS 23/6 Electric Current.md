## 5/11/26

**Amperian loop** - closed loop to measure current through its area

For a uniform current $I$ through an area $\textbf{A}$,
$$
I=nq\textbf{v}\cdot\textbf{A}=\textbf{j}\cdot\textbf{A}
$$
- $\textbf{j}=nq\textbf{v}$ is the **current density** vector, or the amount of charge cariers moving at some drift velocity $\textbf{v}$.

## Ohm's Law

Ohm's law states that
$$
\textbf{j}=\sigma\textbf{E}
$$
- $\sigma$ is the **conductivity tensor** used for **anisotropic crystals**. This is a mechanical property.

### Resistance

The **resistance** is a geometrical and mechanical property of an object.
$$
R=\frac{L}{A}\rho
$$
- $L$ is the length of wire
- $A$ is the cross-sectional area
- $\rho$ is the **resitivity**, or $\sigma^{-1}$

The voltage drop across a resistor is
$$
V=IR
$$
