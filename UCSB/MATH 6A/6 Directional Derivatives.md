## 1/28/26

Let $f:U\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{m}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ be a real-valued differentiable function. The **directional derivative** of $f$ at the point $\vec{p}\in U$ in the direction of the unit vector $\hat{u}$ is given by
$$
D_{u}f=\frac{d}{dt}f(\vec{p}+t\hat{u})\biggr{|}_{t=0}
$$
**Theorem:** gradient directional derivative
$$
D_{u}f=\nabla f\cdot\hat{u}
$$
