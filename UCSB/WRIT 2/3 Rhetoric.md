## Writing a Report

**Who:** Mark Smith
**What:** Murdered
**Where:** Parking garage
**When:** June 6, 2010; 10:37 p.m.
**How:** Multiple stab wounds
### Detective Report

Mark Smith (28M) was found stabbed to death at Lot 100 on 6 June 2010 at 10:37 by a passing witness. He was on the third floor found laying just beyond the elevators with seven stab wounds. The witness was coming through the elevators to get to their car parked on the far end of the floor which was mostly empty. The lab report indicated the murder took place at around 09:30pm with bruise marks indicating a struggle. Nothing strange was reported at the time as no witnesses were nearby.
### Post Mortem Report

Mark Smith is a 28 year old male who died at exactly 9:30pm on June 6, 2010. The examination showed six stab wounds on his hands and lower arms with a fatal one to his chest. Bruise marks on his left shoulder also appear to show evidence of a struggle. We determined the weapon used was likely a 3-inch pocket knife.
### Eulogy

Mark was a great friend. He was a loving husband, and father, and his unexpected passing has brought a lot of shock and distress to our community. I remember when we met at my first job in St. Louis. 
### Prosecution

Your honor, I'd like to start with the defendent's car, which was caught speeding down Stow Ave by parking garage security cameras at 9:38 PM. Apon investigation, a knife was found in the leg space of the back seat matching the post mortem description of the murder weapon. DNA testing undeniably proves the victims blood on it, and I see no other conclusion we can all make.
## Elements

**diction** - choice of words

**jargon** - terminology used in a discourse group to facilitate clear and precise communication

**euphamism** - replacing a word considered offensive, discomforting, or controversial with another term

**colloquial** - language that is informal and usually spoken but not written
- **dysphemisms** - exaggerate rather than soften what could be offensive

**denotion** - literal definition of a word

**connotation** - emotional association with a word
