## 4/22/26

We can spot an academic journal by its citation format refering to the volume number and issue number at the top or bottom of every page.

Research papers are writen by academics for other academics.
- The writer won't always appeal to you and doesn't use any strategies to keep us hooked.
- The writing will also assume prior knowledge that you don't have.

The title typically includes a teaser and a straightforward explanation of the article separated by a comma.
## The Abstract

The goal of an abstract is to 
- encapsulate the main points of the article
- describe the problem or question
- show the approach
- tell the new thing that the article found
- explain why the result is important

The abstract is often skimmed for scholars to determine if they need to seek out that article.
## The Introduction

The introduction serves the same purposes as the abstract with more breathing room. The introduction will also present a road map of the article. Once you figure out the main argument, you can determine how much energy to spend on all the various parts of the reading.
## Section Headings

These are titles for particlar parts. Read all of these to get a sense for the trajectory of the text before reading each section in depth. 
## Conclusion

The conclusion rephrases the main arguments and is where the authors indicate limitations of their work, unanswered questions, and new areas to explore.
## Action
