## Unit Requirements

**units** - credits
- 1 unit: 3-4 hours per week of work
- 184 units required for graduation
- 60 upper division units required for graduation
- 2.0 minimum GPA required for graduation
- 3 quarters of academic residence required for graduation
## First-Year Requirements

- Entry Level Writing Requirement (ELWR)
- American History & Institutions Requirement (AHIR)
## General Education Requirements

**A: English Reading & Composition** (A-F only)
- A1: Writing 2
- A2: Writing 50
**B: Foreign Language**
- 180 units to graduate
**C: Science, Mathematics, & Technology**
- 3 courses
**D: Social Science**
- 2 courses
**E: Culture & Thought**
- 2 courses
**F: Arts**
- 1 course
**G: Literature**
- 1 course
