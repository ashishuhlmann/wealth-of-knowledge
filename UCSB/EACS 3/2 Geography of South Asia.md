## Aryan Invasion

Persian herders and agriculturalist entered from the Northwest Mountain passes (Hindu Kush) in 1500 BCE.
- less technologically developed but used good weapons and chariots

**Aryan Invasion Theory** has been debated by scholars arguing that Aryans were native and that Vedic culture was native to South Asia.
- no archaeological evidence of military invasion by Arya
## Vedic Culture

**Vedic Era** - 1200-600 BCE

Vedics used to memorize liturgies and poetry before writing in **Sanskrit** (Indo-European language) to the earliest Indian scriptures.
1. Rigveda
2. Samaveda
3. Yajurveda
4. Atharvanveda

Vedics labeled universe into three horizontal realms.
1. Earth - humanity
2. atmosphere - birds and chariots of the gods
3. vault of the sky - dieties (devas) and ancestors

**rita** - the principle of natural bodies to move rhythmically and in harmony (also orders the gods)

**propriety** - social order

Gods are intertwined with human life.
1. **Varuna** upholds the rita and punishes those who go against it
	- can spy, listen on conversations, watch
2. **Indra** controls the clouds and rain.
	- warrior and protector of the Arya against external enemies
3. **Agni** is the god of fire.
	- animal offerings are burned for Agni
4. **Surya** is the sun god.


**liturgies** - steps, rituals, and procedures
- Animal sacrifices were for kings, royalty, and big or important people and to entice the gods to come down
- Vegetarianism and non-killing would later be introduced to Hinduism, contradicting earlier Vedic culture.
- emphasized the sounds (words, drums), considering writing to be profane
- **om** - primordial sound of the cosmos
## Classical Hindu Culture

**Vedas** deal with concrete elements of ritual practice: recitation of mantras, benedictions, incantations, rituals steps, ceremonies, and sacrifices.
