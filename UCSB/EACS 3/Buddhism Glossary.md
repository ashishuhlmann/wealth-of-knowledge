**The Four Noble Truths**
1. All of existence is **duḥka** (suffering). (**Suḥka** is pleasure.)
2. The cause of duḥka is **taṇhā** (desire).
3. To end duḥka, eliminate taṇhā.
4. To end taṇhā, follow the **Eightfold Path**

**Dharmacakrapravartana sūtra** - the sūtra setting in motion the wheel of the dharma; scripture containing the Four Noble Truths
- **dharmacakra** - the wheel of dharma
- **cakravartin** - great military leader
- **yāna** - turnings of the dharma wheel
1. **theravāda**
2. **mahāyāna**
3. **Vajrayāna**

**anattā** - no ātman

**The Eightfold Path** - a simultaneous practice with eight aspects

A **prajñā** (wisdom)
1. right views: acceptance of the Four Noble Truths
2. right intention: thoughts of compassion for others; non-violence; selfless detachment
B **śila** (ethical conduct)
3. right speaking: abstaining from lies, slander, abuse, and gossip
4. right action: abstaining from killing, stealing, and sexual misconduct
5. right livelihood: avoiding any ocupation that harms another living thing
C **samadhi** (metnal discipline)
6. right effort: avoiding and unwholesome state of mind
7. right mindfulness: developing selfconscious meditative awareness
8. right concentration: pure equanimity; detached from the illusion of self

**pañcāśila** - The Five Precepts

I resolve to avoid:
1. harming sentient beings
2. taking that which is not freely given
3. sexual misconduct
4. harmful speech
5. intoxicants

**skandhas** - aggregates of personal identity
1. body
2. perceptions
3. emotions
4. karmic dispositions (unconcious motivations)
5. consciousness
- mututally interactive; cause and effect with one another
- nothing stays the same from one moment to the next
- dispassionate observation of myself yields nothing eternal

**three treasures**- seek refuge in the
1. Buddha
2. dharma
3. sangha

**Nirvāṇa** - enlightenment; annihilation of the false idea of permanent self

**aśubha** - revulsion towards the things of this world

**sūnyatā** - doctrine of emptiness; things are empty of own-being, essence, and self-existence

**pratītya-samutpāda** - interdependence of all things

**tathāgata-garbha** - the womb of suchness, generating the cosmos

**yogācāra** - consciousness only; collective mind of all beings

**maṇḍala** - sphere of numerous Buddhas and enlightened beings

**yamabushi** - tradition of solo practice in Japan

**The Vinaya** - monastic code emphasizing practice over doctrine; monastic tradition
- **bhikkhu** - begar; you don't get to choose what you eat to eliminate desire for food
- **bhikkuni** - female monks
- **Tipiṭaka** - three baskets of the Buddhist canon
## Rules of Monastaries

**Pārājika** - four rules resulting in expulsion if violated
1. sexual intercourse
2. taking anything not given
3. depriving another human being of life or inciting self-destruction
4. pretending to have gained insight into the teachings of the Buddha

**Sanghādisesa** - rules requireing a meeting of the community and a period of penancef violated
1. sexual acts
	1. emitting semen (except while sleeping)
	2. touching a woman
	3. addressing a woman with lewd or suggestive words
	4. serving as a romantic go-between for a layman and a laywoman
2. constructing a non-approved abode or meditation hut
3. making false charges against another monk
4. calling attention or taking sides in doctrinal disputes
5. refusing to listen
6. failing to uphold moral and ethical principles

**rules of comportment** - hundreds of rules guiding everyday life in detail

## Buddhism in China (200 CE)

**filial piety** - traditional confusian values clash with monastism
- state control - state is ancient in China checking Buddhist institutional independence

***Tan Sanzang*** - ***Journey to the West***

**The Great Tang Records of the Western Religions** - 19 years of travel describing geography, religions, languages, politics, economies, histories, and climates of the **Silk Road** kingdoms and societies, Central Asia, India, and Sri Lanka
- most valuable measurements and locations of historical templkes and buildings 

**Sutra on the Contemplation of Immeasurable Life** - 1103 CE printed

**Huayan Sect** - founded by Dushun 557-640CE
- all phenomena are interconnected
- **Inra's Net** - infinite net of bright polished jewels
- existence is a vcast body made up of an infinite of individuals all sustaining eachother

**Tianti Sect** - founded by Master Zhiyi
- three vehicles to nirvana
1. hearing the Buddha's sermons
2. own effort
3. through a bodhisattva
- all beings can become Buddhas

**Pure Land Sect**
- appeals to the masses
