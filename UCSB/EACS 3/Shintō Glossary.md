**kami-no-michi** - the Way of the Gods

**kami** - gods; sacred; Shintō is both polytheistic and animistic
1. mythological creators
2. exceptional persons (sacred emperors)
3. extraordinary things (features of the landscape)
4. natural objects and implements 

### Mythological Creators

**kojiki** - the record of ancient things
- **oya-shima-no-kuni** - country of eight islands created in a formless sea
- **Izanagi** - male god creator
- **Izanami** - female god creator

**Goddess Amaterasu**

**The Directive for the Disestablishment of Sate Shintō** - document that separated Shintō from the state in 1945
- separation of Shintō and imperial state
- religious freedom for individuals and communities

**kokoku** - native land

**matsuri** - multi-day festival of song, dance, semi-naked men, handicraft, processions, symbolizing the protection of the community by the local kami

**torii** - symbolic gate; inviting passage from the profane space into the sacred space
- **kuchi-susuru mizu** - water to clense one's hands and mouth before entering the shrine
- **mizuya** - water basin holding the water

**genkan** - entryway to the home; transitional space between the outside and inside
- shoes in a home are profane
- **kamidana** - household shrine

**shintai** - god embodiment

**Meiji Restoration of 1868** - 
- Japan adopts a western-style monarchical constitution
- emeperor becomes divine, descending from the sun goddess Amaterasu and gains a lot of power

**samurai** - warrior class

**religious syncretism** - religions live side by side without hard boundaries
- religious membership and belonging was not exclusive
- community ritual gatherings for Shintō and Buddhist healing traditions

**jingū-ji system** - shrine-temple system; many Shintō shrines have Buddhist temples on the grounds with Buddhist monks invited

**Tendai Buddhism** - extension of the Tiantai Shan sect
- founded by **Saicho** who was a Buddhist monk in the eight century CE
- wanted a single unified Buddhist sect for Japan
- focused on the *Lotus Sutra* and became one of the largest sect
- all Buddhas were bodhisattvas who stayed to help others gain enlightenment

**namu amida butsu** - hail to the Buddha Amitabha

**namu myōhō renge kyō** - devotion to the mystic dharma and of the *Lotus Flower Sutra*

**Zen Buddhism** - austere and ascetic meditative sect
- appealed to the samurai
- monastic sect
- spontaneous enlightment
- physical punishment

**Butsudan** - Buddhist funerary family altar
- home altar
