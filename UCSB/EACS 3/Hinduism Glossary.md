## Introduction

**religion**
- Edward Tylor: "the belief in supernatural beings"
- Bronislaw Malinowisky: religion serves the function of allaying human fears
- in China, the term "religion" came from Japan

**liturgy** - represents a communal response to and participation in the sacred through activities reflecting praise, thanksgiving, remembrance, supplication, or repentance

**collective effervescence** - collective rituals and gatherings concentrate the religious force and produce the powerful feelings of unity, identification, and social solidarity that are necessary to repeatedly and periodically remind people of their belongingness and social obligations, and to produce a good feeling about being together

**social solidarity** - religious feeling, emotions, and rituals that maintain a sense of community identification and belonging

**sacred objects** - religious feeling channeled into key sacred symbols and objects that encapsulate meaning

**structural-functionalism** - the function of religion is to generate social solidarity, avoid modern anomie; the function of sacred objects, places, symbols, and religious activities like rituals or festivals is to produce and maintain society
