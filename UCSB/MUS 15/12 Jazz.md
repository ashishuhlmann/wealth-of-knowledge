Jazz developes in the 1920s with the set of Tin Pan Alley.
- develoeps in New Orleans and Chicago as "America's art form"
- highly segregated musical practice 
- improvisational genre focused on spontaneity
- used in dance, pop, and art music

**bebop** - new musical style in the 1940s and 
- made of two horns (like saxophone or turmpet) and a rythm section of piano, bass, and drums providing a base structure
- fast, blistering tempo; absolute virtuoso musicians
- focus on extended improvisation over set chord changes
- highly evaluated by critics like Amiri Baraka (LeRoi Jones, 1934-2014)
- African American musicians, white critics
