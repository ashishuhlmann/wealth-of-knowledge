## 7/1/26

**canon** - the list of works considered to be permanently established as being of the highest quality and of continued relevance

**absolute music** - music for music's sake without any explicit story attached
- no choreography, costume, or effects; no opera

**programmatic music** - music with an explicit or implied story attached
- opera music
- movie or videogame scores
## Ludwig van Beethoven (1770-1827)

- virtuoso pianist and composer; improviser
- born in Bonn to a family of **court musicians**
- born too late to benifit from aristocratic-court system
- travels to Vienna in 1787

In the 18th century, concerts were primarily social. In the 19th century, concerts did not value social aspects, with an emphasis on individual learning instead. They were not primarily for entertainment; instead, they were for learning (to grapple with, understand, and study the music).

The 19th century saw the formation of all the city philharmonic and orchestras and concert halls.

**monster concert** - enormous concert with tens of thousands

**1869 National Peace Jubilee** - monster concert in Boston
- organized by bandmaster Patrick S. Gilmore (1829-1892)
- 1,000 preformers
- 10,000 choral singers
- 30,000 audience members
- huge temporary halls and coliseums

**1872 World Peace Jubilee** - monster concert in back bay
- 2,000 preformers
- 20,000 choral singers
- 100,000 audience members
