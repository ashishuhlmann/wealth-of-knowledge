**musicking** - musical practices that are shaped by social and cultural forces

**historical thinking** - entails attempting to understand artifacts and experiences produced in the past from the perspectives of the social and cultural forces

Classical music was a functional single-use item. Musical culture reflects social structures, and real social scenarios dictate musical products.

Art is a product of social, temporal, and geographic setting aside from the work itself.
