**early music** - music composed prior to the middle of the eighteenth century
- refers to an approach that is "historically informed"
- both preformance practice and a repertoir

Focuses
1. **period instruments** - historical instruments or historically accurate copies
- doesn't do what a modern instrument do; rhetoric of authentic instuments
2. Early music being performed for its own aesthetic metis, rather than as displayed as some antique oddity
- not trying to recreate the past but make it new and exciting today
3. alternative avenue for musicians not wanting to operate within canonic or "modern" music

## Arnold Dolmetsch (1858-1940)

- English instrumement maker and pioneer in the revival of early music and period instruments
- interest grew in 1890 on old musical instruments
- makes reproductions of historical instruments
- concert tours of early music for people to learn

**organology** - academic study of musical instruments
## Francis Galpin (1858-1945)

- English priest, instrument collector, and organologist
- developes a deep insterest in musical instruments during student years
- Amasses a huge collection (600 instruments)
- explicit belief that instrument collections should also be played and performed
- collection transferred to the Museum of Fine Arts in Boston
## Mary Brown (1842-1918)

- upper-class New Yorker
- wife of a merchant baker John Brown; mother of six
- an "incurable" collector; collects more than 3,600 musical instruments of all periods and cultures
- starts giving her collection in 1889 to the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York
- need to preserve musical instruments for indigenous culture
