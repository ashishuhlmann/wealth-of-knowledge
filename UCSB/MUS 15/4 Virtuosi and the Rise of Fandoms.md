**virtuoso** - one who excels in the technique of an art; a highly skilled musical preformer

- extensive tours; internationally famous

## Niccolò Paganini (1782-1840)

- Italian violinist; most famous violin virtuoso of the early 1800s
- pushes violin technique to new levels
- tours outside Italy from 1828-1834 becoming a celebrity
- too good to be true; frightening in skill; fascination and aversion
## Franz Liszt (1811-1886)

- Hungarian composer conductor, virtuoso pianist, and writer
- composes to showcase his virtuosity
- developes the **solo recital**
- "Lisztomania"
## Johanna Maria (Jenny) Lind (1820-1887)

- Swedish opera swinger known as the "Swedish nightingale"
- internationally famous; tours the U.S. in 1850 through P.T. Barnum (1810-1891)

**P.T. Barnum** - American businessman and producer
- public ticket buying auction in Boston
- Ossian Dodge bid $625 ($24,712 today) for one ticket
