## 7/14/26

**musical exoticism** - the evocation of a place, people, or social milieu that is or imaged to be profoundly different from accepted local norms in its attitudes
- the process of evoking a place that is perceived as different from home by the people who created the exoticist cultural product and by the people who receive it

**tin pan alley** - phrase for the American popular song industry from 1890-1930
- also refers located in New York City (exactly like the term Hollywood)
- neighborhood of music publishing houses
- songs used as social or cultural commentary
- cheap pianos everywhere sounded like kicking a tin pan around
- songs about love, death, technology, and identity

**Kilauea Cyclorama, Chicago World's fair 1893**

*Yaaka Hula Hickey Dula* (Hawaiian Love Song)

sub-industry of Tin Pan Alley for Hawaiian exoticism
- a distant, faraway paradise
- Hawaiian women as objects of desire
- Hula dancing
- ukeleles
- steel guitars
- tropical cuisine
