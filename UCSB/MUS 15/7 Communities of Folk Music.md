## Johann Gottfried Herder (1744-1803)

**volksgeist** - soul or spirit of the people
- manifests itself through language, poems, stories, and songs
- collective voice
- leads to a rise in the prestige of folklore and folk culture
- creates the label of "folk music" in the 1770s through his anthology *The Voice of the Peoples in Songs*
- **volkslied** - folk song
- looked to find uncorupted untainted culture in the rural villages

**nationalism in music** - the use in art music of materials that are identifiably national
- include actual folk music

**nationalism** - the doctrine or theory according to which the primary determinant of human character and destiny is the particular nation to which an individual belongs

## Béla Bartók (1881-1945)

- Hungarian musician, composer, pianist, teacher, ethnographer, collector of folk songs
- listened to a Transylvanian lullaby while on holiday in Hungary
- starts collecting folk songs in 1905 with a phonograph
- "to collect the finest Hungarian folksongs and to raise them, adding the best possible piano accompaniments"
- *Hungarian Folksongs* 1907-1911
- *Fourteen Bagatelles* 1908

**ethnography** - the scientific description of peoples and cultures with their customs, habits, and mutual differences
