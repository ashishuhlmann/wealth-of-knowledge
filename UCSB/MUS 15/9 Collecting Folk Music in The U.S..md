### Huddie William Ledbetter ("Lead Belly," 1888-1949)

- guitarist and laborer
- played twelve-string guitar and accordian
- several jail terms in Louisiana; convicted for murder and sentenced for 30 years
- Lomaxes get enterance to his high-security prison and record folk songs
- myth of his song "Irene" getting him released from prison
- hired by the Lomaxes, accompanying them in their ethnographic studies
- college lectures and preformances on a touring circuit with curation steps
- The Weavers recording of "Irene" became the number one song in 1950
