## 7/2/26

Instruments have be regarded as gendered objects.
## Piano

- keyboard instrument from the 1700s
- **pianoforte** - soft loud; original name for piano; refering to antique pianos
- sign of social standing or piece of furniture
- gendered object

**piano girl** - girls in homes that could play music for the family
- ended as machines like the phonograph entered homes in 1904

The piano girl culture worked to
- limit women's pubic activity
- have women maintain the domestic sphere as their primary social arena
- display or prefrom one's suitability for marriage through musical skill

## Fanny Mendelssohn Hensel (1805-1847)

- composer and pianist
- born in Hamburg, Germany to a wealthy family
- musical activity restricted to private sphere
- **salons** - wealthy social gatherings

## Clara Wieck Schumann (1819-1896)

- child prodigy preforming at the **Leipzig Gewandhaus** at age nine
- born in Leipzig, Germany to a working-class family
- toured Europe extensively
- of the Liszt virtuoso generation preforming recitals and public concerts
