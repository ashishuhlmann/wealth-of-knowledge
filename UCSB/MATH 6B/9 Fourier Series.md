The **Fourier series** representation of $f(x)$ can be written as
$$
f(x)=c+\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}\cos{(nx)}+b_{n}\sin{(nx)}
$$
- $a_{n}$ and $b_{n}$ are the **Fourier coefficients**.
- $\cos{(nx)}$ and $\sin{(nx)}$ are new basis functions.

The Fourier coefficients can be determined by the **Euler-Fourier formula**
$$
\begin{align}
c&=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(x)dx \\
a_{n}&=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(x)\cos{(nx)}dx \\
b_{n}&=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(x)\sin{(nx)}dx \\
\end{align}
$$
