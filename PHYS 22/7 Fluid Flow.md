## Surface Tension

**Surface tension** is the measure of surface force per unit length.
$$
\gamma=\frac{F}{L}=\frac{dU}{dA}
$$
- This means that surface tension is also the surface potential per unit surface area.

**Surface energy** is the energy is the total potential energy on the surface.
$$
E=\gamma A
$$
- $A$ is the total area.

Spheres have the smallest surface-area to volume ratio of any geometric shape, making drops of liquid form into spheres.
## Viscosity

**viscosity** - measure of the resistive frictional forces between particles that take away energy
![[Viscosity.excalidraw]]
$$
F=\eta A\frac{dv}{dy}
$$
- As the temperature in a liquid increases, viscosity decreases.

**laminar flow** - flow of a liquid in which speed changes layer by layer
### Flow through a Pipe
$$
v(r)=\frac{\Delta p}{4\eta L}(R^{2}-r^{2})
$$
- $\Delta p$ is the pressure difference across a length $L$ of the pipe.
- $R$ is the radius of the pipe.

**Poiseuille's law** - The **mass flux** through a pipe is
$$
\frac{dm}{dt}=\frac{\rho\pi R^{2}\Delta p}{8\eta L}
$$

**turbulent flow** - chaotic flow with randomness

The critical average flow speed in a pipe for the flow to become turbulent is
$$
v_{c}=R\frac{\eta}{\rho D}
$$
$$
R=\frac{\rho Dv}{\eta}
$$
- $D$ is the diameter of the pipe
- $R$ is **Reynold's number** which is a dimensionless proportionality constant to characterize any flow

## Brownian Motion

The average area of the square traveled by a large particle in a fluid is
$$
\langle\Delta x^{2}\rangle=\frac{RT}{3\pi\eta aN_{a}}
$$
- $T$ is the temperature.
- $a$ is the radius of the bombarded particle.
- $\eta$ is the viscosity of the fluid.
- $R=N_{a}k_{B}=8.314\frac{\text{J}}{\text{K}}$ is the Boltzmann constant times one mole.
- $N_{a}=6.022\cdot10^{23}$ is Avogrado's constant.
