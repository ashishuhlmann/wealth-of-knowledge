**open circuit** - an open loop of wire with nothing connected
**short circuit** - a closed loop of only wire

**voltage source** - a perfect two terminal element that maintains a fixed voltage across its terminals reguardless of load resistance
- "likes" open circuits
- "hates" short circuits
$$
I=\frac{V}{R}
$$
**current source** - a perfect two terminal element that maintains a fixed current through an external circuit reguardless of load resistance or applied voltage
- "hates" open circuits
- "likes" short circuits
