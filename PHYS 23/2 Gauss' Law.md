## 6/4/26

The total electric flux through a closed surface $S$ only depends on the charge inside the surface.
$$
\oint_{S}\textbf{E}\cdot d\textbf{A}=\frac{Q_{enc}}{\epsilon_{0}}
$$
- $\textbf{E}$ - electric field
- $d\textbf{A}$ - elemental area vector
- $Q_{enc}$ - enclosed charge

If we divde both sides by the enclosed volume and make it really small, this turns into the first of **Maxwell's equations**.
$$
\nabla\cdot\textbf{E}=\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_{0}}
$$
or
$$
-\nabla^{2}V=\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_{0}}
$$
## The Divergence Theorem

**Theorem**: divergence

Let $\textbf{F}:\mathbb{R}^{3}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{3}$ be a vectorfield 
$$
\oint\oint_{S}\textbf{F}\cdot d\textbf{A}=\iiint_{V}\nabla\cdot\textbf{F}dV
$$
