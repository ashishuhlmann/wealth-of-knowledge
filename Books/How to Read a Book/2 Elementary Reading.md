Question: What does the sentence say?

**synthetic method** - teaching individual letter names and sounds, and then combinations to syllables

**phonic method** - teaching sounds that made up words rather than letter names

**visual method** - teaching whole word visual recognition before letter names and sounds

Stage 1: reading readiness (pre-school to kindergarten)
1. physical - good vision and hearing
2. intellectual - minimum level of visual perception and memory
3. language - ability to speak clearly and use several sentences
4. personal - ability to work with others; sustain attention; follow directions

Stage 2: sounds (first grade)
- learning a few sight words
- attribution of meaning to a series of symbols

Stage 3: vocabulary (elementary school)
- rapid vocabulary progress
- unlocking meaning through context clues
- discovery of purposes to reading

Stage 4: refinment (middle school onward)
- improving of all the previous skills
