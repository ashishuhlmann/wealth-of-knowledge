Question: What is the book about?

**skimming process** - inspection within a few minutes to an hour on what the book is about
1. Look at the title page and preface.
2. Study the table of contents.
3. Check the index.
4. Read the publisher's blurb.
5. Glance through the most important chapters.
6. Turn all the pages, reading a paragraph or two here and there.

**superficial reading** - reading a book through for the first time without stopping to look or ponder at anything confusing

**structural note-making**
- What kind of book is it? What is it about as a whole?
- What is the structural order of the work whereby the author develops his conception or understanding of that general subject matter
- notes made on the contents page

Inspectional reading should be fast.
